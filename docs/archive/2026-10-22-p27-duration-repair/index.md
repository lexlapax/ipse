measurement parsers previously limited the entire sequence to one day. That
cannot compose fourteen legal maximum-length services plus cleanup. A new
public Freeze regression fails on the original source with `execution_limits`.

protocols one shared fifteen-day maximum. Freeze uses the same finite ceiling.

root allowance is `14 * L + 918000` ms. At the legal service maximum this is
86430000 ms per cell and 1210518000 ms for the root, or 14 days, 15 minutes,
18 seconds. Ordinary stdio remains capped at 24 hours. SDK-2 extraction and
reranking remain bounded at two minutes with reasoning disabled.
