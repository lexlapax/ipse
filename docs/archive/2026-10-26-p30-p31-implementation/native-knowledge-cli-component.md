## Behavior and authority boundary

`knowledge enable` imports the actual bundled native notes artifact, admits the
chosen folder/repository identity and displays the complete protected policy.
Only a fresh action/code response through the matching native input/display
channel can save permission. The host rechecks actual installed artifact,
sidecar, recipes, routes, registry identity and expected consent epoch after
review. A changed epoch refuses instead of overwriting another review.
Cancellation can leave the inert import and project admission, but grants no
new permission. Ordinary command-line approval flags cannot bypass this flow.

The saved activation applies to the next new session. It authorizes the two
literal/pending managed capture recipes and their bounded collection reads,
including headless collection effects, without widening shell/workspace/MCP or
delegation approval. Main inference retains its existing route/accounting;
the policy permits zero secondary model calls. The reviewed Main timeout is
at most 90 seconds and cannot widen the configured timeout. Daily collection
limits are 100 operations, 2 MiB input and 4 MiB output; the grant expires in
one year. These are finite defaults for this native preset, not qualified
intake or capacity measurements.

`status` checks fresh readiness against actual artifacts/configuration without
joining a worktree or repairing protected state. `permissions` shows the saved
policy even when the selected route no longer admits it; its readiness field
is null. Both use the independently repaired existing-only readers. `disable`
requires a separate native review and exact consent epoch. Revocation preserves
stored records/receipts and does not cancel effects already admitted.
