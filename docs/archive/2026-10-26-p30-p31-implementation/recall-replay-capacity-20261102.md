# Bounded replay computation, 2026-11-02

This development increment follows the [33-note native recall diagnostic](recall-many-native-20261102.md). It reduces repeated pure replay work; it does not qualify the native recall journey or change the current 32-note selection limit. The [plan](../../plan.md#p31-usable-knowledge-and-prompt-assistance) records the remaining work.

The original synthetic source contained 33 separately reviewed notes. Its saved reconstruction took about 4.9 seconds in two fixture measurements; separate component timings put conversation context reconstruction near 3.2 seconds and source-window reconstruction near 15 milliseconds. A profiling run showed 561 lineage capacity checks encoding about 80.6 MB in total, of which about 73.6 MB consisted of unchanged fields. The Erlang profiler wrote its report but its temporary launcher then exited with a stop-status assertion mismatch; the reported call counts are diagnostic, not a completed benchmark gate. An earlier attempt to reload an instrumented host module before Session activation was refused and did not measure a handoff.

`Saved` now scopes two bounded scratch caches to a single projection. `ManagedLineage` reuses exact JSON sizes for unchanged capacity fields, retaining at most 64 signatures and 4 MiB of signature bytes; `ManagedReference` reuses at most 128 exact dependency hashes, with each encoded key bounded to 8 KiB. The caches use deterministic term bytes, including signed-zero distinctions, and are discarded or restored on scope exit. They cache neither source eligibility nor a lease. Every dependency validation and the existing byte/count bounds remain in force; protected uses still reacquire the original source and policy owners.

The [comparison script](recall-replay-equivalence-20261102.exs) ran the original `830a12c` modules and the updated modules against the same retained synthetic journal. Context and saved-history fingerprints matched after excluding only the fresh source-window secret and window ID. One sequential local sample measured context at 3.08 seconds before and 2.27 seconds after, and saved reconstruction at 4.03 seconds before and 3.42 seconds after. These are local samples with no latency or capacity qualification.

The actual native 32-note baseline control still stopped before provider dispatch, with ten delivered worker inputs followed by an unknown result and unconfirmed cleanup (seed 791924). A later control with explicit cache-memory bounds also stopped before dispatch after 13 delivered inputs (seed 791927). A subsequent scope-cleanup refinement changed only restoration of a pre-existing process-dictionary value; the large native diagnostic was not rerun after that refinement. These samples do not establish a reliable number of completed handoffs, prove the cause of every failure or justify increasing a deadline. The full fitting-set path, a solution to repeated source reconstruction, source-loss/owner-loss qualification and the complete P31E gate remain open.
