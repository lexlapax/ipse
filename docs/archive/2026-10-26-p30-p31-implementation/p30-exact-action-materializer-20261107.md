# Synthetic exact-action assessment materializer

Recorded 2026-11-07 as scoped development-source evidence. The [plan](../../plan.md)
owns current milestone status; [decision engines](../../developer/decisions.md)
explains the host contract. This record does not establish P30 completion.

A synthetic assessment owner binds its `decision.create` fence to the selected
parent identity, descriptor, arguments and complete expected request. The request
includes the frozen rubric. The Session journals the fence attempt and the
ordinary action, and saved reconstruction validates the recorded relationship.
This fence is specific to that decision action; it is not a general restriction
on all Session actions or network egress.

A host-sealed current-source headless run compiled the selected source and
passed 18 pure checks, two policy checks and seven decision Session tests; 15
other decision tests were excluded. Its independent audit found no members of
recorded process groups, open files under that run root or holder of the
operator-input lock at inspection. Four event outcomes and several invocation
outcomes remain unknown. All seven roots and their original lock inodes remain
retained; nine valid release receipts do not settle those unknown outcomes.
The headless run did not exercise the terminal materializer.

The later materializer V4 used a fresh isolated synthetic fixture, normal native
terminal approval and loopback services. Its strict compile exited zero for 202
modules, and its single test passed in 18.1 seconds. The test checks that a
validly re-declared changed rubric refuses before Session execution, observes no
connection to the selected loopback listener during a bounded check, and compares
session-file contents and consent snapshots. For the positive path it checks
one matching fence attempt, its parent and exact approved request, a valid `ok`
action result, same-ID saved reconstruction, frozen first row, and rejection
of synthetic labels as independent evidence. It records seven synthetic main
requests across seed and assessment and one settled decision request. Quality,
whole-task cost and timing remain unverified; there is no qualified default.

The V4 launcher records matching pre/post source, runtime and toolchain
inventories and empty recorded stage process groups. An independent read-only
audit observed no recorded group members, open scratch files, lock holders or
selected listener at inspection. It checked the two retained journals' matching
action, invocation, request and worker-input records and verified three
release receipts against their intents. The fixture and original lock inodes
remain retained. These point-in-time observations do not prove that every
detached process stopped or settle unrelated unknown effects. The source and
runtime inventories are not an operating-system or network seal.
This run did not access the declined retained notebook or an equivalent recall
consumer. It does not qualify live provider access, installed behavior, real
input quality, whole-workload egress or the final integrated regression.

A subsequent HEAD-based P30-only candidate excluded the interleaved retirement
and provider-carryover changes. Its isolated build passed, along with 36 focused
checks, seven decision Session controls (15 excluded) and one fresh synthetic
terminal materializer test. Elixir 1.18/OTP 27 formatting and a clean-snapshot
documentation check also passed. An independent audit verified the recorded
materializer receipts and observed no remaining related processes, listener or
lock holders. Four interrupted Session event results and several invocation
outcomes remain unknown; their seven roots and original locks are retained. This
focused candidate still needs the full applicable baseline before handoff.

older host inventory. Its captured inventories matched the separately reviewed
updated host seal; the stopped run and scratch remain separate evidence. V3
compiled successfully but its materializer test failed after the synthetic
assessment had run: the test expected a rubric-refusal file that the integrated
recipe did not create. The subsequent repair restored the missing negative
control and V4 used a new source commitment and a fresh fixture. The V3 failure
and its effects remain separate; do not resume or replay its fixture.

were removed after an incomplete audit had checked unknown event results but
had not accounted for invocation intents without results. This removed three
Session lock inodes and three artifact-import lock inodes; two removed roots had
unmatched invocation intents. Twenty-one copied journals, receipts and selected
artifacts had been byte-verified, and six release signatures later verified,
but copies do not restore inode identity or establish missing outcomes. Do not
recreate those locks, resume those Sessions, replay the invocations or classify
their outcomes as known. Four other roots with explicitly unknown event results
and their original locks remain retained.
