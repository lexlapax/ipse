## Keep selection distinct from acceptance

The [frozen contract](baseline.md#confirming-a-proposed-decision) requires a bounded
fully displayed selection with an immutable manifest and explicit acceptance.
`knowledge review KEY KEY [...]` freezes two to eight distinct selected candidates
in an at-most-8-KiB manifest. Initial signed reads must share one root and current
project/binding/policy/recipe. The manifest preserves exact text, record revision
and value hash, source class/origin/version/provenance, signed clocks and proposed
accepted-category retention. Parsed shapes and hashes provide no source or
terminal authority.

Each candidate still uses ordinary public native review with its own fresh
`accept CODE`, reservation, journal, current source/Consent/target checks and
one-use final publication. The complete frozen manifest appears with each current
card and is persisted in its accepted event. No permit is derived from another
item's code, and argv selection never grants acceptance. The actual card must
match its selected manifest member. Earlier own publications may advance the root;
a changed candidate refuses instead of replacing the frozen member.

An optional closed `batch` member carries the manifest, digest and index. Existing
cards/events remain readable without it. Manifest-bearing cards use the already
available native Review bounds of 16 KiB of facts and 64 KiB of escaped display;
the manifest remains at most 8 KiB. Single-card review keeps its original 8-KiB
display bound. No global/native quota, Rust protocol, dependency, public SDK or
provider choice changes. Core host changes are necessary for exact native
attestation and persisted acceptance reconstruction; a worker cannot provide them.

Every accepted item incurs its own operation and measured input/output; the full
manifest adds journal/input bytes. Cancellation, refusal or uncertainty stops
the rest. Earlier confirmed items remain saved; remaining candidates retain their
pending clocks. Unknown effects keep their existing full liability and never
replay. Operation inspection correlates the manifest hash, batch ID, item index
and count while retaining per-item status and the current-eligibility disclaimer.
