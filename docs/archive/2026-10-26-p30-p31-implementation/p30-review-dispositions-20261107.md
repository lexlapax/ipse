latency or benefit. The [paired critical observation](p30-selection-paired-critical-20261106.md)
exposes known per-case membership losses but does not establish independent

The [assessment materializer](candidate-assessment-materializer-20261106.md)

scorer correctly rejected the explicitly non-independent synthetic labels.
That refusal is not a negative quality result. Pending-only observations cannot
demonstrate reduced false-durable capture, and the measured experiment's
whole-task time and cost remain null.

## Remaining review and qualification

The finite design permits **qualified/useful**, **evaluated and rejected/no
benefit**, or **unverified** outcomes for each experiment. A completed negative
evaluation can close an experiment without a qualified default, but a scripted
fixture or missing account access is neither a completed negative evaluation
nor evidence that an engine is unavailable. See the [frozen P30 contract](baseline.md#p30-interfaces-and-useful-consumers).

Qualifying a strategy or reporting a measured negative requires the applicable
predeclared matched inputs and engine-free baselines, independent label custody
and review, frozen first predictions, whole-task timing and total cost, plus the
protocol, source, permission, budget and integration gates. The scorer's hashes
and supplied reviewer fields do not prove truth or independence. Broader installed
operator use and later document/graph routing have separate evidence gates.
An unverified account can remain an explicit limitation in a reviewed local scope;
it does not waive the remaining local baseline, integration, documentation or
milestone checks.
